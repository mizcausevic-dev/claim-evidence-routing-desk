# Kinetic Gain Embedded Tie-Back

`claim-evidence-routing-desk` is the Insurance / InsurTech proof surface for missing packet routing, denial pressure, stale evidence, and appeal-safe sequencing.

## What it demonstrates

- claim packet intake and routing posture
- owner-safe escalation for missing clinical, coding, and appeal proof
- denial prevention through packet completeness visibility
- operator-readable appeal readiness without exposing live payer systems

## Why this matters to Kinetic Gain Embedded

Embedded products increasingly need claim-aware and evidence-aware operator surfaces inside customer workflows. This repo shows the reusable primitive:

1. ingest structured claim and packet exports
2. analyze missing proof, stale packets, and workflow drift
3. present one buyer-readable control surface for operators, reviewers, and admins

The same architecture can be embedded into SaaS products that need:

- reimbursement workflow visibility
- denial-prevention analytics
- packet-completeness scoring
- safe internal review tooling for insurance operations
